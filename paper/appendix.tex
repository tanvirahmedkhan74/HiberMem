\section{Mathematical checks}
\label{app:math}

\paragraph{Pure interactions and Shapley.}
For the unanimity game \(u_T(S)=\ind[T\subseteq S]\), players outside \(T\) are
dummies. Players in \(T\) are symmetric, and efficiency requires their Shapley
values to sum to \(u_T(N)-u_T(\emptyset)=1\). Hence
\(\phi_i=1/|T|\) for \(i\in T\). A pure AND chain is therefore not a
counterexample in which necessary records receive zero Shapley value.

\paragraph{Range.}
An order-\(k\) discrete derivative contains \(2^k\) bounded terms with alternating
sign. At most \(2^{k-1}\) positive-signed terms can equal one while negative-signed
terms equal zero, and vice versa, so
\(|\Delta_Tv(S)|\le2^{k-1}\) for \(v\in[0,1]\). The same applies to
\(a(T)=\Delta_Tv(\emptyset)\).

\section{E3d frozen-design summary}
\label{app:e3d}

E3d uses development base banks 360--361 and verification base banks 370--373.
Matched single-/dual-path ledgers cross AND2/AND3, low/high lexical overlap,
base/counterfactual worlds, two query roles, and targeted evidence panels. The
current contract, one query-anchored label-only contract, and one structured
diagnostic run in development; only the label-only candidate can run in
verification or enter E4. Existing .95 supported/format and .05 cap/unsupported
criteria are unchanged, with full-memory, exact-support, counterfactual,
other-query-capture, single-to-dual-drop, and per-link checks added. The full
preregistration is included in the supplemental repository.

\section{Reproducibility and audit boundary}
\label{app:repro}

The repository's independent validators reconstruct manifests, condition counts,
row identities, symbolic support, rewards, analysis summaries, and cryptographic
artifact links. Real inference requires committed source and a pinned model
revision. Future capability access is denied until a matching immutable freeze
exists. Mock artifacts are explicitly engineering-only. Historical Phase-2 test
state is outside E3d/E4 and remains locked.

Before any confirmatory claim, the following must be committed: exact prompts and
parsers; model/tokenizer revision; hardware and numeric precision; generation
parameters; bank/query generators and IDs; split capabilities; coalition sample
design; policy set and tie rules; cost accounting; endpoint; practical margin;
bank-level test; multiplicity rule; variance-only pilot; sample size; stopping rule;
and independent validator. Partial calls are neither successes nor failures and must
resume with identical identities.

\section{Reviewer-facing failure criteria}

\begin{enumerate}
    \item No E3d label-only contract passes: stop E4 for this reader/task pair.
    \item E4 shows no practical advantage or an item baseline wins: reject H1.
    \item Interaction predictors do not improve future-mask prediction: reject H3
    for the tested shift.
    \item Sign-aware lesion damage is null, reversed, or imbalanced: reject H2.
    \item Controlled gains do not replicate on a second model and natural memory:
    restrict the contribution to a synthetic diagnostic.
    \item Estimation cost dominates achieved savings: reject deployment relevance.
\end{enumerate}
