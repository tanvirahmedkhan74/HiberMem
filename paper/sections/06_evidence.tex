\paragraph{E3c grounding decomposition.}
For the current contract, supported AND2/AND3 accuracy is .917969/.935547,
unsupported assertion is .243815/.220145, strict formatting is .776001, and the
generation-limit rate is .187012. The answer-slot intervention raises strict
formatting to .997070 and reduces the limit rate to .000366, but lowers supported
accuracy to .843750/.880859 and raises unsupported assertions to
.331380/.433594. At memory cardinality seven, its unsupported-assertion rate reaches
.50 for AND2 and .667 for AND3.

This pattern rejects a tempting shortcut: constraining presentation does not ensure
evidence fidelity. If a retention mask preserves all but one link and the reader
completes the path from priors or competing records, raw correctness can reward a
policy for unsupported guessing. Support-conditioned accuracy, abstention, and
competing-query capture must therefore accompany the primary outcome.

\paragraph{What has not been shown.}
No real prospective mask has been selected and evaluated; no interaction-aware
method has beaten exact Shapley or another strong baseline; no sign-aware lesion has
run; no natural conversational-memory result exists; and no second-model replication
exists. The complete past games are exact observations under their fixed reader,
not ground truth for future utility.
