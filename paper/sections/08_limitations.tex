\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limitations}

\paragraph{Evidence boundary.}
The central tests are unrun. A reader-readiness failure is a useful warning but not
a retention contribution. Model and contract choices were developed after earlier
failures; only fresh verification and confirmation can support generalization.

\paragraph{Scale.}
Complete eight-record games require exponential calls and permit exhaustive subset
selection. No current result supports sparse recovery, candidate generation, or
approximate optimization at realistic bank sizes. The cost of measuring a game may
exceed the context savings it enables.

\paragraph{Construct validity.}
The controlled tasks use synthetic directed links with known support. Natural
memories contain ambiguity, contradiction, stale facts, evolving preferences,
retrieval error, and open-ended outputs. Availability interventions also change
prompt length and local presentation; equal-cardinality and payload controls reduce
but cannot eliminate prompt-mediated effects.

\paragraph{Interaction semantics.}
Different indices encode different axioms. A fitted low-order coefficient can be an
omitted-order projection rather than a stable mechanism. Exact past quantities do
not establish future causal structure. We therefore require predictive validation,
basis sensitivity, and matched lesions.

\paragraph{Biological analogy.}
Artificial hibernation in mice preserves memory and associated neuronal
representations despite broad synapse loss, with retained synaptic architecture
associated with persistence \citep{lin2026hibernation}. This motivates a general
question about structure under turnover but supplies no mapping from synapses to
text records, no estimator, and no evidence for \systemname{}. We make no claim of
biological fidelity.
