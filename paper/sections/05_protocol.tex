\section{Leakage-safe protocol and hypotheses}
\label{sec:protocol}

\paragraph{Capabilities and stages.}
Each bank has disjoint past and future query capabilities. Policy code accepts a
restricted past-evidence object containing only bank ID, query ID, coalition mask,
and reward. It cannot receive support labels, generated outputs, or a future-query
object. The execution state is:
\[
\textsc{Past}\ \longrightarrow\ \textsc{Validate}\ \longrightarrow\
\textsc{Freeze}\ \longrightarrow\ \textsc{Future}\ \longrightarrow\
\textsc{Validate}.
\]
The freeze binds source, configuration, model, tokenizer, prompts, runtime,
manifests, evidence hash, predictor, and every selected mask. Future execution is
denied before a valid freeze. Mock evidence is marked engineering-only and cannot
qualify a method.

\paragraph{H1: prospective retention.}
For severe budgets \(k\in\{2,3\}\), a past-only low-order interaction selector
improves future original accuracy over every prespecified strong item method, with
exact-Shapley top-\(k\) as the primary comparator. For bank \(b\),
\begin{equation}
D_b=\frac12\sum_{k\in\{2,3\}}
\left(A^F_{b,\mathrm{interaction},k}
-A^F_{b,\mathrm{exact\ Shapley},k}\right).
\label{eq:primary}
\end{equation}
A practical margin and confirmation size will be frozen after a variance-only pilot
on separate banks. No current result estimates Equation~\ref{eq:primary}.

\paragraph{H2: matched lesion.}
On a reserved cohort, a deletion with larger past-predicted \(G(D)\) causes more
future damage than a matched control. Pair identities and balance tolerances are
frozen before future outcomes. Negative-interaction and zero-separation placebo
lesions test sign and residual confounding.

\paragraph{H3: incremental prediction.}
On a method-independent panel of future masks, the past-only \(d=2\) or \(d=3\)
predictor improves error, rank correlation, and top-set overlap beyond \(d=1\).
We distinguish surface-paraphrase transfer (H3a), compositional/dependency shift
(H3b), and later temporal shift (H3c). The current E4 generator tests H3a only.

\paragraph{Measurement readiness.}
Before any hypothesis test, the reader must satisfy frozen supported-accuracy,
strict-format, generation-limit, and unsupported-assertion criteria. Full-memory,
exact-support, counterfactual tracking, competing-query capture, and every
missing-link position are also checked. A structured path certificate may diagnose
reasoning, but cannot silently replace the label-only E4 interface.

\paragraph{Inference.}
The independent unit is the base memory bank. Queries, worlds, masks, and seeds are
nested repeated observations. The frozen primary procedure is a one-sided
bank-level sign-flip/randomization test with a bank bootstrap interval; mean,
median, positive-bank fraction, and all \(D_b\) are reported. Exact enumeration uses
the exact tail proportion; Monte Carlo randomization uses an add-one correction.
Secondary contrasts are multiplicity-controlled.
