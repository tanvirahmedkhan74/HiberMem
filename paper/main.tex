\section*{Reproducibility statement}
The accompanying repository records exact configurations, source and runtime
identities, raw generations, resumable condition tables, symbolic controls,
independent validators, and immutable phase transitions. Appendix~\ref{app:repro}
states the audit boundary and the items that must be frozen before prospective
inference. Completed evidence is distinguished from mocks and proposed experiments.

\section*{Ethics statement}
The completed controlled experiments use synthetic identifiers and no human-subject
or personal data. Natural conversational benchmarks are proposed only for later
external validation and will require a separate privacy, licensing, and
LLM-as-a-judge audit. A primary research-integrity risk is overstating diagnostic or
adaptively selected evidence; our protocol therefore reports failed screens and
prevents development artifacts from unlocking confirmation.

\section*{AI use statement}
